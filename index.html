Floor Plan

The lab network

The same network you finished CCNA 1 with: R1, R2, S1, S2, PC-A, and SRV1, with the same 192.168.10.0/24 and 192.168.20.0/24 you subnetted and pinged. It grows one step a week. Every session page says what was added.

CCNA 1 callback R1 and R2 got replaced over the break. The 2911s you configured in CCNA 1 are now ISR4321s, the router NetAcad's own Packet Tracer activities use in this course. Same jobs, same cables, new interface names: G0/0 becomes G0/0/0 and G0/1 becomes G0/0/1. Session 1 walks through it.

The OSI Company presents

DISPATCH

The OSI Company's second series, starring Local Dispatch, the Layer 2 couriers who move frames around the floor. The Rookie, the Surveyor, and the NOC are back from The Zoning Office. Two arcs. In Floor Plans, Dispatch redraws the building with VLANs, trunks, a loop-proof hallway plan, and bundled corridors.
